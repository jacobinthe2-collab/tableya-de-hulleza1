About Tableya De Hulleza

Heritage cacao, homegrown warmth.

Tableya De Julyeza brings the taste of Filipino tableya to your table. Our cacao is carefully prepared, boiled low and slow, it is inspired by the simple comfort of our childhood.

Handcrafted tableya

Cacao tablets are made from roasted beans and packed with bold Filipino flavor.

Locally sourced

We support local farmers and use only the finest Philippine cacao beans.

Family recipes

Each dish is inspired by recipes passed down across generations.
